Delete your account

You can request deletion of your SpillTheReel account and its associated data, even if you have uninstalled the app.

Request deletion by email

Write to the developer, Aditya Jadhav, at adityajadhav0723@gmail.com. Ask to delete your SpillTheReel account and send the request from the email address associated with it, if you can. We may ask you to verify ownership before acting. Leave passwords and verification codes out of your message.

Delete from the app

Go to Account → Delete account and follow the confirmation steps. If you want to keep a copy of your available data, use Export my data first.

What happens to your data

Account deletion removes your access and starts background cleanup of associated data, including your saved library, app-stored media and derived content, reminders and notification registrations. It does not delete posts on the original social platform or copies you exported.

Some limited records may be kept as needed for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations or backup recovery. Backup copies expire according to the applicable backup lifecycle. Read the Privacy Policy for more about retention and the services that process data.
