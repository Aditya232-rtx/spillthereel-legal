Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

This policy covers the SpillTheReel Android beta and its account services. SpillTheReel is operated by Aditya Jadhav. Contact the developer about privacy at adityajadhav0723@gmail.com.

Information we use

  • Account. We use your email address, account ID and any name or profile image you provide to sign you in and manage your account. Our authentication provider handles password sign-in. If you use Google sign-in, we receive the account information Google shares to complete sign-in.
  • Saves and imports. When you save a link or select an export to import, we receive the link or selected file. We may retrieve media and related information such as captions and creator details, and generate thumbnails, transcripts, summaries, categories and search representations so you can find and use your saves.
  • Ask and feedback. We process questions you submit and relevant saved content to retrieve results and generate answers. The app stores chat history on your device. If answer reporting is available and you send a report, we receive the reported answer or excerpt, your report reason and your account ID for review.
  • Reminders and notifications. We use schedules and preferences you set to deliver reminders and updates. If you enable push notifications, we register a device push token to send them. You can turn off notifications in the app or device settings.
  • Saved places and nearby search. A saved place may contain a venue name, city and address from saved content or your edits. If you grant location permission and choose Nearby, the app uses your location to identify nearby saved places. The app sends derived city names to the SpillTheReel API for matching. Your device's location and geocoding services may process location as part of that lookup.
  • Service operation and diagnostics. Requests provide technical information such as network and request metadata. We use this information to run the service, limit abuse and investigate faults. When enabled, crash reporting can receive diagnostic and device or app information. Optional app analytics can associate app interactions with an account identifier.

Services that process data

We use Supabase for authentication, database and file storage, and Google Cloud to host the service and run processing tasks. Configured content retrieval services such as Bright Data, Apify or ScrapeCreators may receive links that you save. AI services such as Google Gemini and transcription services such as Groq may receive the content or excerpts needed for processing. When enabled, Expo and Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver notifications, Sentry helps diagnose crashes, and PostHog supports app analytics.

Opening a source post or a venue in another app takes you to that provider. Its privacy policy applies to your use of that service. We may also disclose information when required by law.

How we protect and retain data

The beta uses HTTPS to communicate with its hosted services. Access to saved libraries is scoped to the signed-in account. We use access controls for service credentials and private storage.

We keep the information needed to provide your saved library and account features while you use the service. Temporary processing files and import data may be kept for processing, retry or recovery and removed when they are no longer needed for those purposes. Some records may be retained as needed for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations or backup recovery. Backup copies are removed through the applicable backup lifecycle.

Export or delete your data

Use Export my data in Account to save a copy of your available data. You can remove individual saves from your library.

To delete your account and associated data, go to Account → Delete account in the app. If you no longer have the app, request deletion without the app.

Account deletion starts a background cleanup process. Some limited records may remain for the purposes described above. Copies you exported to your device and content held by the original social platform are controlled separately.
